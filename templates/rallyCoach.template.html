__TITLE__

Print pair cards. Partner A solves while Partner B coaches, then switch.

How to play RallyCoach
  1. Partners sit side by side with the card set.
  2. Partner A reads the left task and writes the answer.
  3. Partner B watches and coaches (no writing yet).
  4. Partners check the answer (fold open).
  5. Switch roles for the next card — Partner B writes, Partner A coaches.
