__TITLE__

Print one worksheet per student. Walk around, find a classmate who can answer each cell, write their name.

How to play Find Someone Who
  1. Stand up with your worksheet and a pencil.
  2. Find a classmate. Ask them the question in one cell.
  3. If they answer correctly, write their name on that cell.
  4. You may not use the same classmate twice in a row.
  5. Try to fill all cells!
