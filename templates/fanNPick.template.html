__TITLE__

Print numbered cards. Students fan cards, one picks, one reads, one answers, one coaches.

How to play Fan-N-Pick
  1. One student fans the cards face-down.
  2. Student 2 picks a card by number.
  3. Student 3 reads the question aloud.
  4. Student 4 answers.
  5. Student 1 coaches or praises, then roles rotate.
