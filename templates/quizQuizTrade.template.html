__TITLE__

Print, cut along dashed lines, fold each pair in half. Question on front, answer on back.

How to play Quiz-Quiz-Trade
  1. Each student gets one folded card.
  2. Stand up, hand up, pair up with a classmate.
  3. Partner A reads their question. Partner B answers.
  4. Partner B reads their question. Partner A answers.
  5. Trade cards and find a new partner.
