How to play Showdown
  1. Team captain reads the card question aloud.
  2. Everyone writes their answer face-down (no peeking).
  3. Captain says "Showdown!" — everyone flips their answer.
  4. Team checks answers. Coach anyone who got it wrong.
  5. Next card — rotate captain.
